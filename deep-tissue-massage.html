Break Down Knots & Chronic Tension

When standard relaxation massages don’t penetrate deeply enough into rigid, locked muscle groups, Deep Tissue Massage at Walnut Spa in Naubad, Bidar delivers the exact therapeutic firmness your body needs. It targets the sub-layers of muscle fibers and the protective connective tissue (fascia) surrounding them.

Therapeutic Protocol

The 5-Step Deep Tissue Protocol

1

Pain Zone Mapping

Identify focal tension zones (upper back, rhomboids, lumbar, sciatic, or gluteal areas).

2

Thermal Tissue Warming

Effleurage with warm eucalyptus and camphor oils to soften superficial fascia fibers.

3

Cross-Fiber Friction

Deliberate slow stripping perpendicular to muscle fibers to break painful knot adhesions.

4

Trigger Point Compression

Sustained direct static pressure on neuromuscular trigger points to force muscle release.

5

Cooling Hydration

Warm herbal towel compression to soothe tissues followed by pure electrolyte hydration.
