# Walnut Spa & Wellness - Naubad, Bidar

A warm-themed luxury spa web experience crafted for **Walnut Spa**, the premier wellness and therapy sanctuary in Naubad, Bidar (Karnataka).

---

## 🌿 Key Features & Design Highlights

- **Warm Luxury Aesthetic**: Tailored color palette of rich cream, warm sand, gold accents, and deep forest green with glassmorphic cards and subtle shadows.
- **Top Services Arched Carousel**: Custom arched pill cards (`border-radius: 125px`) with smooth dragging, arrow controls, and responsive swipe support.
- **Complete 12-Treatment Catalog**: Full coverage of all wellness services (Aroma Massage, Full Body Spa, Thai Spa, Deep Tissue, Swedish, Couple VIP Suite, Jacuzzi, Herbal Steam Bath, Signature Ritual, Balinese, Reflexology, and Dry Sauna) with real-time category filtering.
- **Interactive Service Detail Modals**: Deep dives for each treatment featuring benefits, focus areas, and direct booking actions.
- **Smart WhatsApp Booking Engine**: Real-time form that composes an automated, pre-formatted WhatsApp booking message directly to `+91 9972053939`.
- **Atmosphere Gallery & Lightbox**: Interactive photography showcase of executive suites, private therapy rooms, and amenities with fullscreen preview.
- **Zero External Dependencies**: Pure semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS design system, and vanilla JavaScript.
