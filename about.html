Our Vision

Crafted for Those Who Value True Rest

In a fast-growing city like Bidar, daily responsibilities, business commutes, and long working hours often leave the body physically strained and the mind exhausted. Walnut Spa was founded with one clear mission: to provide an authentic, hygienic, and deeply relaxing sanctuary where you can pause, breathe, and restore your inner balance.

Located conveniently on the First Floor above HP Gas in Naubad (right beside Gurudwara Arch), our facility is designed with spacious private air-conditioned suites, attached hot-water glass showers, fresh linens for every guest, and soothing ambient lighting that immediately slows your pulse.

The 4 Pillars of Our Excellence

What sets Walnut Spa apart from ordinary massage parlours in Karnataka.

Certified Trained Therapists

Our therapists (both female and male) are formally certified in human anatomy, therapeutic acupressure points, and professional hygiene protocols.

Hospital-Grade Cleanliness

Every therapy suite is sanitized between guests. We use fresh, crisp sterilized linens, disposable items, and medical-grade disinfectants.

Pure Organic Botanicals

We never use synthetic mineral oils. Only cold-pressed almond, sesame, and coconut base oils infused with pure therapeutic grade lavender and lemongrass essences.

Complete Private Suites

Every suite features sound insulation, temperature control, attached private showers, and complete discretion for your peace of mind.

Jacuzzi & Steam Amenities
