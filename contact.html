Travel Guide

Driving Directions From Your Area

How to reach Walnut Spa Naubad from every major neighborhood across Bidar.

From Gandhi Gunj

Distance: 3.8 km • ~7 mins

Head north on Gandhi Gunj Main Rd toward Naubad Highway. Continue straight past Gurudwara Arch; Walnut Spa is on the 1st floor above HP Gas.

From Shivnagar

Distance: 3.2 km • ~6 mins

Take Shivnagar Main Road towards Janwada Road junction, merge onto Naubad Road. Turn right beside Gurudwara Arch.

From Bidar Fort / Old City

Distance: 4.5 km • ~9 mins

Drive past Chaubara Tower toward Mohan Market, proceed along the main bypass north directly to Naubad.

From Mailoor / Chidri

Distance: 5.5 km • ~10 mins

Follow Chidri Road toward Ring Road, take the direct flyover route towards Naubad Gurudwara Arch.

From Mohan Market / Udgir Rd

Distance: 2.9 km • ~5 mins

Head north along Udgir-Bidar Road straight into Naubad commercial zone. Look for HP Gas on your right.

From GNDEC & Janwada Rd

Distance: 1.8 km • ~4 mins

Exit Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College campus, head west onto Naubad road. Arrive in under 4 minutes.
